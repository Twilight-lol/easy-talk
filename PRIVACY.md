# Easy Talk privacy policy

**The full, current privacy policy is at [twilightstudio.dev/easy-talk/privacy](https://www.twilightstudio.dev/easy-talk/privacy/).** That page is the official version; this file is a summary and links to it so the two can't drift apart. The [Terms of Service](https://www.twilightstudio.dev/easy-talk/terms/) are there too.

Easy Talk: Voice Dictation (Android package `app.easytalk`, and the Windows app) is published by Twilight Studio Technologies LLC, an Ohio limited liability company doing business as Twilight Studio.

## In short

- **No accounts, no Easy Talk servers.** We don't receive your voice recordings or the text you dictate.
- **Online mode:** your recording, the transcript, your dictionary words, the writing style for the app you're typing in and, for Voice Edit, the selected text and your instruction go directly from your device to the AI service you chose, with your own API key. That service handles them under its own privacy policy.
- **Windows:** speech recognition is online by default when you've entered a Groq or OpenAI key (or your own server). Set the speech engine to Local to recognise speech on your PC, so only text is sent.
- **Offline mode (Android):** speech recognition, clean-up and translation run on your phone. Offline translation and language detection use Google ML Kit, which sends Google technical performance information (not your text).
- **On your device only:** history, settings, dictionary, snippets, stats, API keys (encrypted on Android), the optional Voice Lock voiceprint, and recordings that failed to transcribe (on Android, the 5 newest, kept until you retry or delete them). The Android app is excluded from cloud backups.
- **Pro purchases (Android):** Google Play processes payments. RevenueCat, our service provider, starts when the app opens and receives a random install ID (your "Easy Talk ID"), your Google Play purchase records for Easy Talk, and technical details such as app and Android version, country and IP address.
- **Rewarded ads (Android):** only when you tap "Watch 2 ads". Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location), device information and ad interactions. Where a privacy law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland, regulated US states), Google's consent or opt-out message appears first, and you can change your choice in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teenagers.
- **Downloads and updates:** models come from GitHub, Hugging Face and Google; the Windows app downloads speech models from the Handy project's host and checks GitHub for updates.
- We don't sell your personal information, and the app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.
- Easy Talk is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices and requests
