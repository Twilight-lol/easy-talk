# Easy Talk

Voice dictation for **Android** and **Windows**. Talk, and clean, properly punctuated text appears wherever you're typing.

Easy Talk: Voice Dictation is published by Twilight Studio Technologies LLC ([twilightstudio.dev](https://www.twilightstudio.dev/projects/easy-talk/)).

## Get it

- **Android:** on **Google Play** (`app.easytalk`). Google Play is the only Android version that gets updates.
- **Windows:** download `Easy.Talk_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` from [Releases](https://github.com/Twilight-lol/easy-talk/releases/latest). Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**. The app checks this page for updates after that.

## What it does

- **Dictate anywhere.** On Android, tap the floating bubble. On Windows, hold **Ctrl + Space**.
- **AI clean-up** fixes grammar and punctuation, removes "um"s, applies corrections like "at 2… actually, make it 3", and formats lists, money, times and email addresses.
- **Your dictionary and snippets:** names and jargon spelled right, and "my email" types your full address.
- **Offline mode (Android):** download the models, and speech recognition, clean-up and translation run on your phone with no internet.
- **Local speech recognition (Windows):** set the speech engine to Local and speech is recognised on your PC; only the text is sent to your AI service for clean-up. With a Groq or OpenAI key and the engine on Auto, speech recognition runs online.
- **16 languages** in the Android app.
