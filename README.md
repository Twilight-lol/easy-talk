# Easy Talk

Voice dictation for **Android** and **Windows**. Talk, and clean, properly punctuated text appears wherever you're typing.

## Get it

- **Android:** coming soon to **Google Play** (in closed testing now).
- **Windows:** download `Easy.Talk_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` from [Releases](https://github.com/Twilight-lol/easy-talk/releases/latest). Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**. The app updates itself after that.

Older Android versions are also on the Releases page as `EasyTalk-x.y.z-android.apk`, but the Play Store version is the one that gets updates.

## What it does

- **Dictate anywhere.** On Android, tap the floating bubble. On Windows, hold **Ctrl + Space**.
- **AI clean-up** fixes grammar and punctuation, removes "um"s, applies corrections like "at 2… actually, make it 3", and formats lists, money, times and email addresses.
- **Your dictionary and snippets:** names and jargon spelled right, and "my email" types your full address.
- **Offline mode:** speech recognition and clean-up that run entirely on your device.
- **16 languages** in the app.

## Privacy

No account and no Easy Talk servers. Your voice goes only to the AI service you chose, or nowhere at all in offline mode. Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md), or how to [delete your data](DELETE-DATA.md).
