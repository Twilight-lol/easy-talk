# Delete your Easy Talk data

Easy Talk (Android app by Twilight_lol on Google Play) has no accounts and no Easy Talk servers. Almost everything stays on your phone, so deleting it is in your hands.

## Data on your phone

Your settings, dictionary, snippets, history, stats, API keys, Voice Lock voiceprint and downloaded models are stored only on your device.

**To delete them:** uninstall Easy Talk, or go to Android **Settings → Apps → Easy Talk → Storage → Clear data**. Everything is deleted at once and nothing is kept.

You can also delete single items in the app: History (delete one or all), Words (dictionary and snippets) and Settings → Offline mode → Delete models.

## Purchase records (RevenueCat)

If you bought or restored Easy Talk Pro, RevenueCat keeps a record linked to a random install ID (not your name or email) so your purchase can be restored.

**To ask for it to be deleted:**
1. Open Easy Talk → **Settings → Easy Talk Pro → Copy my Easy Talk ID**.
2. Open a request at [github.com/Twilight-lol/easy-talk/issues](https://github.com/Twilight-lol/easy-talk/issues/new?title=Delete%20my%20data) titled "Delete my data" and paste the ID. You don't need to include anything else about yourself.

The RevenueCat record is deleted within 30 days. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases and payments under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); manage it at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com). Deleting the RevenueCat record does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the Play Store first.

## Ad data (Google AdMob)

If you watched a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may have collected your advertising ID and related data under [Google's ad policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Easy Talk never receives it. To delete or reset your advertising ID: Android **Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID**.

## Voice and text sent to your AI service

Recordings and text sent for clean-up, translation or Voice Edit go directly to the AI service you chose (Groq, OpenAI and so on) with your own key. Easy Talk does not keep them. To delete data held by that service, use its own account settings.
