# Delete your Easy Talk data

Easy Talk: Voice Dictation (Android package `app.easytalk`, by Twilight Studio Technologies LLC) has no accounts and no Easy Talk servers. Almost everything stays on your device, so deleting it is in your hands. The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://www.twilightstudio.dev/easy-talk/privacy/#retention-and-deletion).

## Data on your phone

Your settings, dictionary, snippets, history, stats, API keys, Voice Lock voiceprint, recordings waiting to be retried and downloaded models are stored only on your device.

**To delete all of it:** uninstall Easy Talk, or go to Android **Settings → Apps → Easy Talk → Storage → Clear data**. Everything is deleted at once.

You can also delete single items in the app: History (one item or all), Words (dictionary and snippets), failed recordings (delete them where they're listed), and **Settings → Offline mode → Delete models**.

## Purchase records (RevenueCat)

RevenueCat, which confirms and restores Pro purchases for us, keeps a record linked to a random install ID (your "Easy Talk ID"), not to your name or email. It includes your Google Play purchase records for Easy Talk.

**To ask for access to it or its deletion:**
1. Open Easy Talk → **Settings → Easy Talk Pro → Copy my Easy Talk ID**.
2. Email the ID to **hello@twilightstudio.dev** with the subject "Delete my data". You don't need to include anything else about yourself. Please don't post your ID in a public GitHub issue.

We verify the request and respond within the deadlines set by the privacy law that applies to you. We delete or anonymise the data we're not required to keep; transaction records that tax, accounting or other laws require us to keep are kept only for that purpose and period. After deletion, Pro may not be restorable. Deleting this data doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it in the Play Store first. Google Play keeps its own purchase and payment records under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); manage them at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Ad data (Google AdMob)

If you watched a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may have collected your advertising ID and related data under [Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Easy Talk never receives it. To change your ad choice: **Settings → About → Ad privacy choices** (where shown). To delete or reset your advertising ID: Android **Settings → Google → All services → Ads**.

## Voice and text sent to your AI service

Recordings and text sent for speech recognition, clean-up, translation or Voice Edit go directly to the AI service you chose (Groq, OpenAI and so on) with your own key. Easy Talk does not keep them on any server. To delete data held by that service, use its own account settings.

## Windows

The Windows app stores its history, recordings, settings and API keys on your PC. Delete recordings in History, or uninstall the app and delete its app data folder to remove everything.
